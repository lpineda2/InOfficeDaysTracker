Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Does it track me all day?

No. The app only checks whether you are inside your designated office geofence. It does not record your location history or movements.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no email required. Just download and set your office location.

Can my employer see my data?

No. All data is stored locally on your iPhone. There is no server, no admin dashboard, and no way for anyone else to access your information.

Does it work with multiple offices?

Currently the app tracks one primary office location. Support for multiple offices is on the roadmap.

How much battery does it use?

Minimal. Geofencing uses iOS built-in low-power location monitoring. You will not notice a difference.

Is it really free?

Yes. The app is free to download and use with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases.

Privacy Policy

In Office Days is designed with your privacy as the top priority. All attendance and location data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to any server or cloud.

  • No accounts: You are never asked to sign up or log in.
  • No cloud sync: Your data never leaves your device.
  • No tracking: The app does not track you outside your chosen office location.
  • No ads or analytics: There are no third-party trackers, ads, or analytics SDKs.
  • Full control: You can delete your data at any time from the app settings.

If you have any questions about privacy, please contact support.
