What's New in Version 1.15.1:

This update refines the weekly tracking experience introduced in 1.15.0.

• Attendance Trend now matches your tracking cadence. With weekly tracking enabled, the chart groups your office days by week and shows a reference line for your weekly goal, so you can see at a glance how each week compares to your target.

• Average Duration is clearer. Instead of showing "0h" before you've finished your first visit, the card now says when there's nothing to average yet. Weekly trackers also see an all-time average rather than one that resets each month.

• History no longer shows a monthly goal when you're tracking weekly, so your dashboard and history always tell the same story.

Monthly tracking is unchanged.

Thank you for using In Office Days Tracker!
