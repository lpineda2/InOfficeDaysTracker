What's New in Version 1.15.0:

Version 1.15.0 adds Weekly Hybrid Policies.

You can now track weekly in-office expectations, including a minimum number of office days per week and anchor-day rules such as requiring at least one Monday or Friday. This week's compliance status and next-step guidance appear right on your dashboard.

To enable weekly tracking:
Settings → Goals & Policy → Tracking Cadence → Weekly or Both

Monthly tracking remains the default for existing users, so nothing changes unless you choose to enable weekly tracking.

Thank you for using In Office Days Tracker!
