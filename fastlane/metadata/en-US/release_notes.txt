What's New in Version 1.17.1:

• Location setup is fixed. "Use Current Location" now asks for location access when it's needed, instead of showing an error.

• The app now tells you when automatic tracking can't work. If location access is missing, limited to While Using, or Precise Location is off, a card on the dashboard and a new Location Access section in Settings explain what's needed and take you straight to Settings.

• Weekly reminders. If you track by week, you'll get a notification after a visit when your week needs attention, such as when you still need to come in on a required day.

• Turning off notifications now also cancels reminders that were already scheduled.

• Fixed "Today" and "Yesterday" labels in Recent Visits that could be out of date after midnight.

Thank you for using In Office Days Tracker!
